# Fiqh rule verification — Musafir Guidance

Checked 8 Nov 2026 against published secondary sources (fatwa sites and guides that cite the
classical texts). Not yet checked against the classical texts themselves or by a scholar.
Status key: **Confirmed**, **Confirmed with caveat**, **Not verified**.

## 1. Distance threshold

| Madhhab | App value | Status | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hanafi | 77 km | Confirmed with caveat | Four opinions in Radd al-Muhtar (63 / 54 / 48 / 45 miles); the majority prefer 48 miles = 77.25 km [S1]. Derivation: 4 barid = 16 farsakh = 48 English miles [S2]. Scholars using the sharʿi mile give ≈ 95 km; the Deobandi elders (Gangohi, Thanvi, Kifayatullah) meant English miles [S3]. |
| Maliki | 81 km | Confirmed | 16 farsakh / four burud ≈ 81 km, measured one way [S9, S11]. |
| Shafiʿi | 81 km | Confirmed | Two marhalah = 4 burud = 16 farsakh = 48 Hashimi miles; ≈ 81 km common, al-Zuhayli ≈ 89 km [S10]. |
| Hanbali | 81 km | Confirmed | 16 farsakh / four burud ≈ 81 km, measured one way [S9]. |

Also confirmed:
- All four schools: concessions begin only after leaving the town's built-up area [S10, S11].
- Hanbali, Maliki, Shafiʿi measure the distance "only in the direction of journey", not round trip [S9]. The app's straight-line figure is a conservative lower bound.

**App change (done):** Hanafi note gives 77 km majority and up to 95 km with the sharʿi mile; overall range 77–95 km.

## 2. When a stay makes you a resident

| Madhhab | App rule | Status | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hanafi | ≥ 15 nights | Confirmed with caveat | Sources say "fifteen complete days and nights" in a single place [S4–S7]. Travel status ends only by intending 15+ days in ONE place, so hopping between towns under 15 days each keeps traveller status [S8]. The app counts nights as a proxy. |
| Maliki | ≥ 4 full days, excluding arrival and departure | Confirmed with caveat | Malikis, Shafiʿis, Hanbalis: four days [S12]; Mughniyya gives "four days" for Malikis and Shafiʿis [S9]. Yasir Qadhi: Malikis and Shafiʿis differ over when the count starts and ends and whether arrival/departure days count [S12]. A Maliki scholar should confirm the app's treatment. |
| Shafiʿi | ≥ 4 full days, excluding arrival and departure | Confirmed | "Less than four full days, excluding the day of arrival and departure"; Monday→Saturday = four full days [S13, S10]. App's `nights − 1 ≥ 4` matches. |
| Hanbali | > 4 nights; exactly 4 = Borderline | Confirmed | "A period during which more than 20 salats become wajib" [S9]; "twenty prayers, not four days" [S12]. Four nights can hold 21 prayers depending on arrival time, so Borderline is correct. |

**Undecided stays (gap, now noted in the app):** Shafiʿi — up to 18 days, excluding arrival and departure, only for someone whose need could be met at any moment (e.g. waiting for a caravan or the weather) [S13]. Hanafi — remains a traveller until firmly intending 15 days in one place [S8].

**App change (done):** Hanafi label "15 days or more"; note on undecided stays.

## 3. Status of qasr

| Madhhab | App text | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Hanafi | Qasr is obligatory for the traveller. | Confirmed — "necessary (wajib) to shorten" [S5]. |
| Maliki | Qasr is a stressed sunnah. | Consistent with sources (confirmed sunnah) [S11]; read as summary only. |
| Shafiʿi | Qasr is permitted, and better on long journeys. | Confirmed — Minhaj: better at three marhalah, completing better on shorter qualifying journeys [S10]. |
| Hanbali | Qasr is preferred. | Consistent with sources (afdal) [S11]; read as summary only. |

## 4. Combining (jamʿ)

| Madhhab | App rule | Status | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hanafi | Not permitted, except ʿArafah and Muzdalifah | Confirmed | Only jamʿ ṣūrī otherwise [S11, S13]. |
| Maliki | On the road, or when there is a need; not during an ordinary stay | Confirmed | Permitted mainly while actually moving and for hardship; when encamped, generally avoided unless there is a need such as illness or rain [S11]. Malik, al-Shafiʿi and Ahmad all permit taqdim/taʾkhir while travelling [S9]. |
| Shafiʿi | Permitted (taqdim or taʾkhir) while still a traveller | Confirmed | [S9, S10, S11] |
| Hanbali | Permitted; better to pray each on time when settled | Confirmed (first part) | Permitted moving or encamped, according to need [S11]. The "better on time when settled" wording was not found in a source. |

**App change (done):** Maliki jamʿ text adds "or when there is a need such as illness or rain".

## 5. Shafiʿi jamʿ conditions (niyyah card)

Confirmed [S10, S13]: taqdim — pray in order, intend to combine before or during the first prayer, no long gap; taʾkhir — intend to delay while the first prayer's time is still in; Hanafis do not combine outside Hajj rites.

Not verified today (standard Shafiʿi positions, no page reached states them): taqdim — still a traveller when starting the second prayer; taʾkhir — still a traveller until both are finished.

## 6. Not verified — needs a scholar or another pass

1. **Arabic niyyah wording.** No source found. Highest-risk item. Suggested references: Iʿanat al-Talibin, Fath al-Muʿin, al-Nawawi's Minhaj.
2. **"Farthest point from where the journey began" for multi-stop trips.** A reviewer's engineering rule. The classical test is the distance one *intends* to travel to a known destination [S8]; no source addresses zig-zag itineraries directly.
3. **Maliki "20 prayers" vs Shafiʿi "4 full days".** Whether the two counts start and end the same way (§2).
